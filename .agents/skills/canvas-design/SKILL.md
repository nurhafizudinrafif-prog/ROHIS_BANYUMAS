# Canvas Design Skill

These are instructions for creating design philosophies and visual artworks, emblems, posters, and graphics.

Complete this in two steps:
1. Design Philosophy Creation (.md file)
2. Express by creating it visually (.svg, .png, or canvas code)

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY CREATION

Create a VISUAL PHILOSOPHY that will be interpreted through:
- Form, space, color, composition
- Images, graphics, shapes, patterns, emblems
- Minimal text as visual accent

### THE CRITICAL UNDERSTANDING
- What is created: A design philosophy/aesthetic movement.
- Expressed visually: 90% visual design, 10% essential text.
- Emphasize craftsmanship REPEATEDLY: The final work should appear as though it took countless hours to create, was labored over with care, and comes from someone at the absolute top of their field.

### HOW TO GENERATE A VISUAL PHILOSOPHY
- **Name the movement** (1-2 words): e.g. "Geometric Prestige", "Banyumas Heritage"
- **Space and form**: Structural alignment, balance, hierarchy
- **Color and material**: Harmonic calibrated palettes, gold accents, deep contrast
- **Scale and rhythm**: Macro and micro visual elements
- **Composition and balance**: Asymmetrical tension or sacred symmetry
- **Visual hierarchy**: Guiding the viewer's eye naturally
